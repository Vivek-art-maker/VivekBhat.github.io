About me

Vivek is a market research professional with 3+ years of experience across the FMCG and Banking sectors, including over 2 years in dedicated insights functions. He specialises in shopper analytics, brand tracking, survey design and category insights using syndicated research tools, and is skilled at translating data into actionable business recommendations.

Vivek has worked with organisations including Numerator, Innova Market Insights, HDFC Bank and Kantar, on projects spanning household panel data, brand tracking, ad testing and consumer behaviour research for FMCG and personal care brands. He holds an MBA in Marketing from Mumbai University, and is comfortable working across Excel, SPSS, R, Power BI, Tableau and Qualtrics.
